# Product Vision

## Mission

AI Book Loop assists an author in writing a book chapter by chapter while preserving the author's creative intent and maintaining continuity across the whole book.

## Core interaction

The author provides a theme, inspiration or lore, plus optional constraints and ideas. The system helps transform that input into an outline and then into chapters, with explicit author approval between major stages.

## Core product principles

- The author remains the source of creative intent.
- Generation is incremental and chapter-scoped.
- Continuity is treated as a first-class concern.
- Generated material is reviewed before becoming canonical.
- The system should remain provider-configurable and cost-conscious.
