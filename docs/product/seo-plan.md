# Book Loop — SEO product positioning

## Objective

Make search engines associate Book Loop with its actual product vision:

> **Écrire avec l’IA sans perdre la cohérence d’une histoire longue.**

Book Loop should not be positioned as a generic AI book generator. The distinctive concept is the **Canon narratif**: a durable reference for the story that constrains generation, supports verification and keeps the author as the final authority.

## Core positioning

- Primary message: **Écrivez avec l’IA. Gardez votre histoire cohérente.**
- Product category: atelier d’écriture IA pour les histoires longues.
- Differentiator: Canon narratif + vérification de continuité + validation humaine.
- Trust message: **L’IA propose. Vous décidez ce qui devient canon.**

3. Qu’est-ce qu’une bible narrative ?
4. Comment construire une bible de roman ?
5. Comment éviter les contradictions dans un roman ?
6. Comment gérer les personnages d’un roman ?
7. Comment écrire une série de romans avec l’IA ?
8. IA et écriture : comment garder sa voix d’auteur ?
9. ChatGPT peut-il écrire un roman cohérent ?
10. Comment corriger un chapitre généré par une IA ?

Each guide should answer the reader's problem first, demonstrate the concept of Canon with concrete examples, and only then connect it to Book Loop.

## Technical SEO baseline

- Canonical URL generated from `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL`.
- `robots.txt` generated by Next.js and excludes private application areas.
- `sitemap.xml` contains public routes only.
- Open Graph and Twitter metadata describe the product positioning.
- `SoftwareApplication` and `WebSite` JSON-LD are present on the homepage.
- Public pages should receive page-specific titles/descriptions as they are added.
- Authenticated/private routes must remain non-indexable.
- Do not expose manuscript text, Canon data, prompts or AI responses in metadata or structured data.

## Acquisition funnel

`Search → useful guide → continuity problem → Canon concept → Book Loop → free start`

The SEO strategy is therefore designed to create category association, not just traffic volume.
